# 60-second demo plan

| Time | Visual and narration |
|---|---|
| 0–10s | Show a CSC-style desk and the form. Say: “Filling one pension form can mean repeating questions and typing every answer. Solli lets an applicant say it instead.” |
| 10–40s | Show Solli asking a question aloud, then a fake Tamil-English answer. Show transcription and the field filling. Include one correction: “Change village to [fake village],” then show that field update. |
| 40–50s | Show the completed fake-data summary, read-back, confirmation, and printable PDF preview. State: “The operator checks it before printing; Solli does not submit it to a government portal.” |
| 50–60s | Close: “Solli is a voice form-filler prototype for CSCs and panchayat helpdesks, built with Gnani AI models.” Show `#GnaniAI #GreatIndianAIInternshipChallenge`. |

## Demo safety checklist

- [ ] Use fake data only.
- [ ] Do not show a real Aadhaar number or phone number.
- [ ] Do not expose an API key, `.env`, terminal environment, or account screen.
- [ ] Obtain the applicant/actor’s permission before recording.
- [ ] Do not claim a government partnership, submission capability, or verified accuracy.
