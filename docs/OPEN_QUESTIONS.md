# Open questions

| UNCLEAR item | Required action |
|---|---|
| Source form | Provide a public blank PDF or clear image in `forms/source/`; confirm it may be committed. |
| Form metadata | Verify exact title, issuer/scheme, printed languages, page count, and fillability. |
| Every row in `FORM_FIELDS.md` | Verify exact printed label, existence, requiredness, validation, and field order. |
| Every row in `FORM_LAYOUT.md` | Verify page, position, line/box/tick control, and character-box count. |
| Date, phone, account, Aadhaar | Verify actual format and any split boxes; do not assume a rule. |
| Gender | Verify printed choices and tick-box behaviour. |
| Non-spoken elements | Identify signatures, photos, attachments, declarations, and office-use-only boxes once the form exists. |
| Tamil wording | Native speaker review of every question, help prompt, and read-back. |
| Scope | Confirm the supplied form does not require portal submission, document verification, eligibility decisions, or real Aadhaar verification. |
