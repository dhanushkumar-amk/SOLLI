# Problem statement

## One sentence

Solli helps CSC and panchayat helpdesk staff guide an applicant through one old-age pension form by voice instead of repeatedly typing answers.

## Three sentences

Paper forms can be difficult for elderly and first-time rural applicants to complete without help. CSC and panchayat helpdesk operators often repeat questions, type answers, and check details manually. Solli is a planned voice-guided workflow for one old-age pension form: it records Tamil-English mixed answers, prepares the form for review, and creates a printable PDF without submitting it to a portal.

## 120-word version

Paper forms can be difficult for elderly and first-time rural applicants to complete without help. At a CSC or panchayat helpdesk, an operator may need to repeat questions, type each answer, and check details before printing. Solli is a prototype for that assistance flow. It will ask questions aloud, accept Tamil-English mixed spoken answers, and prepare one old-age pension application for review. The operator or applicant can hear the answers, correct unclear details, and create a printable PDF using fake data in the demo. Solli is designed for organisations that assist applicants, not for direct government submission. It will not store personal data, verify Aadhaar, make eligibility decisions, or replace the operator. The official form remains TBD in Phase 4.

## Why now / why voice

The target workflow already depends on a conversation between an applicant and an operator. A voice-guided interface can keep that conversation centred on the applicant while making each captured answer visible for review. ## Who it helps

### Deployer: CSC, panchayat helpdesk, NGO, or bank branch

Example: a panchayat helpdesk uses one shared phone or computer to guide applicants through the same form and print a reviewed copy.

### Operator: front-desk staff member

Example: an operator starts a session, repeats a question only when needed, corrects a misheard village name, and checks the PDF before printing.

### End applicant: elderly or first-time rural applicant

Example: an applicant answers in Tamil-English mixed speech, listens to the read-back, and asks for one detail to be changed before the form is printed.
