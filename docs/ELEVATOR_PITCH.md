# Elevator pitch

## 30-second version

Solli means “say it” in Tamil. It is a voice form-filler prototype for CSCs and panchayat helpdesks helping elderly or first-time rural applicants with one old-age pension form. The applicant answers in Tamil-English mixed speech, while Solli is planned to transcribe the response, show the field, read the completed form back, and prepare a printable PDF. Prisma handles speech to text, Timbre handles read-back, and Evon is optional for ambiguous extraction. The operator remains in control, and no personal data is stored.

## 10-second version

Solli helps a CSC operator guide an applicant through one pension form by voice, then review and print it without storing personal data.
